Building a Multi-Agent Financial AI System

By Amirmahdi Ghasabioroji on August 4, 2026 03:20 PM (UTC)

I spent this year assembling a 4-agent pipeline — Analyst, Risk, Strategy, and Execution — orchestrated with LangGraph and backed by RAG over Qdrant. The interesting part was not only the agents, but the evaluation layer: Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, drawdown, and a 5,000-path Monte Carlo bootstrap. 126+ tests later, the system is less of a demo and more of a habit: measure, stress-test, then trust.
